Difficulty Curve (Phase 5.16) — Endgame Pressure Multipliers

Structural difficulty knobs that compound on top of the NN's continuous factors. HP multiplier kicks in at W20 (+5%/wave, cap 4×) — applied AFTER the NN's hp_mult so a strong checkpoint pushing 3× still gets the ramp on top. Leak damage scales every 10 waves so late-game leaks drain the base much faster (W11→2 HP, W21→3 HP, W31→4 HP per leak).
